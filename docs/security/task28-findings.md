# Task 28 — Security findings register

Assessment baseline: `main` commit `ff290289488378ff1b70ebabac66f3fefa0da937`; remediation branch `task/28-security-privacy-audit`.

Severity meanings for this register:
- **Critical:** direct compromise or broad unauthorized disclosure/control with severe impact.
- **High:** material risk to patient/staff data or core security boundary; blocks release.
- **Medium:** meaningful weakness with bounded exploitability/impact; must be fixed or explicitly accepted before production.
- **Low:** defense-in-depth or operational improvement.

## Closed / remediated findings

The code-level remediations for SEC-28-001, SEC-28-002, SEC-28-009, SEC-28-010, SEC-28-011 and SEC-28-013 were retested successfully in CI run #862. ## Release decision

**BLOCK RELEASE.** SEC-28-003 remains high and open: several data categories still lack approved retention rules, and the approved audit-log backup pipeline is implemented in this branch; CI/deployment evidence and other retention categories remain open. Patient/CRM records and original audit logs are now owner-approved for indefinite retention and must not be automatically purged. SEC-28-012 (staff MFA) remains high and open pending a specific factor and recovery policy. SEC-28-005 automated scans have passed; supplementary secret-surface review remains open at medium severity. Medium findings SEC-28-004, SEC-28-006, SEC-28-007, SEC-28-008 and SEC-28-014 also remain open. No critical finding was confirmed from the reviewed source, but this is not equivalent to a complete penetration test.

Do not test production or third-party systems without written authorization. Do not use real patient data or credentials in CI.
